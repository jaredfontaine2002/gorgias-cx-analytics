# Notes

## The SLA targets are the defaults for a reason

4 hours first response, 48 hours resolution — those are the targets in
`analyze.py` because they're the ones shops actually run. If your shop
runs different numbers, change the two constants; don't change them just
to make the breach chart look better.

## Sizing being top volume is a content problem

When sizing questions are your biggest tag, that's not a staffing
problem. One good fit guide and an auto-reply macro take a chunk of them
before a human ever sees them. Staffing fixes the tickets you should be
getting; content fixes the ones you shouldn't.

## Coverage and warranty

The 10:00 peak is where I'd put coverage first — that's when the queue
is built, and a slow start there follows you all day. Warranty is the
slowest tag because it needs a triage step (serial, proof of purchase,
photos) before it can reach an agent who can actually decide anything.
Send it straight to an agent and it just bounces.
